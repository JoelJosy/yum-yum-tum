The dosa is too good. Its both soft and crispy at the same time, and the masala is yum too. Although it looks small, it’s real filling. They don’t serve sambhar though by default but apart from that it’s great. 

Note that there may be a queue to enter. It does get quite long sometimes but its well worth the wait. Can’t go wrong with this one.
